# Platform Engineering Product Teams（Adopt）

"Platform engineering product teams" are internal teams that create platforms enabling other teams to deploy and operate systems with reduced complexity. They function as "just another product team, albeit one focused on internal platform customers."

Critical success factors:
- "Clearly defined customers and products" must exist before implementation
- Teams should apply "the same engineering disciplines and ways of working as any other (externally focused) product team"
- Use concepts from Team Topologies for organizational structure

What to avoid:
- Simply renaming existing teams "platform teams" without changing actual practices
- Creating "layered platform teams" that preserve technology silos
- Establishing separate DevOps teams as an alternative
- Ticket-driven operating models

Evolution: the concept progressed from "Assess" status (2017) to "Trial" (2020) to "Adopt" (2021+), reflecting increasing industry maturity and proven benefits in "accelerat[ing] application development, reduc[ing] operational complexity and improv[ing] time to market."

# Miscellaneous Platform Teams（Hold / Proceed with Caution, 2022-03-29）

Thoughtworks previously endorsed "platform engineering product teams" as an effective operating model for internal platforms, enabling delivery teams to self-service deploy and manage systems with minimal lead time and reduced architectural complexity.

However, the analysis reveals a problematic trend: the "platform team" label applied to teams dedicated to projects that don't have clear outcomes. These unfocused platform teams struggle with excessive cognitive load and misaligned priorities while managing unrelated systems, essentially functioning as catch-all support units.

The organization maintains its conviction that "platform engineering product teams focused around a clear and well-defined (internal) product offer a better set of outcomes" compared to miscellaneous alternatives that accumulate unwanted or ill-fitting projects.
