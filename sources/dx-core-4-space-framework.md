## DX Core 4 Framework Overview

The DX Core 4 is a unified framework for measuring developer productivity which combines DORA, SPACE, and DevEx. The DX Core 4 framework captures developer productivity through four dimensions: speed, effectiveness, quality, and impact.

## Key Capabilities

- Unified Definition: creates a shared definition of productivity by consolidating DORA, SPACE, and DevEx into one integrated approach
- Benchmarking: organizations can benchmark organization and team performance against hundreds of organizations worldwide
- Multi-Level Analysis: drill down to the team level while keeping an eye on high-level organizational trends
- Actionable Insights: measure both inputs and outputs — understand both what's happening and how to improve

Notable customer quote: the framework is "like getting a blood panel or CAT scan into the engineering organization."

Official development: DX Core 4 was developed by Laura Tacho (DX CTO) and Abi Noda (DX Co-founder and CEO), in collaboration with the authors of DORA, SPACE, and DevEx (who are DX advisors): Dr. Nicole Forsgren, Dr. Margaret-Anne Storey, Dr. Thomas Zimmerman, and others.

## SPACE Framework Context

The SPACE framework takes a broader view of productivity through five dimensions: Satisfaction, Performance, Activity, Communication & collaboration, and Efficiency & flow. However, it told you the categories but didn't tell you which specific metrics to track in each one. Most teams that tried SPACE abandoned it within a quarter because defining your own metrics from scratch is genuinely hard.

## DX Core 4 Metrics

The framework has four primary dimensions with metrics for each category: Speed measures how quickly teams can deliver code changes, including PRs per engineer, lead time, and deployment frequency. Quality measures the stability and reliability of software in production, while Impact measures how much developers contribute to business value beyond just writing code.
